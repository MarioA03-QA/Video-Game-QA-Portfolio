# SESSION-002 — Total War: Rome II Campaign & UI Exploration

## Game

Total War: Rome II

## Platform

PC

## Game Version

2.4.0

## Testing Session

### Duration

60 minutes

### Test Area

Grand Campaign

### Testing Objective

Explore and observe Total War: Rome II's campaign and user interface systems, with a focus on campaign navigation, army management, settlement management, diplomacy, resource information, turn progression, and battle interaction.

## Testing Approach

The session was carried out using the method of exploratory testing rather than predetermined test cases over for 60 timed minutes.
The testing focused on interacting with various campaign and user interface systems whilst looking out for unintended, unexpected, inconsistent, or incorrect behaviour.
The following areas were covered:

- Campaign map navigation
- Army management
- Settlement management
- Character management
- Diplomacy
- Resource management
- Technology and research
- Turn progression
- Battle interaction
- User interface and menus



Exploration Log

### Exploration 1 — Campaign Map Navigation

**Action:**

Navigate around the campaign map using the mouse and interact with different regions, settlements, armies, and map elements.

**Observation:**

The campaign map, alongside all of its interactable elements such as the different regions, settlement and armies, are all functional, respond correctly to mouse inputs. No unexpected or unresponsive behaviour was observed.

**Result:**

Normal Behaviour



### Exploration 2 — Army Management

**Action:**

Select an army and explore the available army management options, including moving the army, viewing its units, and interacting with available army controls.

**Observation:**

Available armies can be moved, managed and interacted with correctly. Unit information can also be viewed successully without any unexpected visual issues.

**Result:**

Normal Behaviour



### Exploration 3 — Settlement Management

**Action:**

Select a controlled settlement and inspect its available buildings, construction options, public order, population, and other settlement information.

**Observation:**

Controlled settlements can be explored successfully. Available buildings and construction options are displayed corerctly and are available for selection (subject to available resources). Public order, population and other settlement information such as cultures  are displayed correctly.

**Result:**

Normal Behaviour



### Exploration 4 — Character Management

**Action:**

Inspect a general or other character and explore the available character information, attributes, traits, skills, and management options.

**Observation:**

Generals or other characters such as the royal family correctly display the characters' available information, traits, skills and management options such as requesting a divorce funtion correctly, without any visual or interface issues.

**Result:**

Normal Behaviour



### Exploration 5 — Diplomacy

**Action:**

Open the diplomacy interface and inspect relationships with other factions. Explore available diplomatic actions such as trade agreements, non-aggression pacts, alliances, or declarations of war where available.

**Observation:**

Diplomacy interface opens correctly and allows the player to view current relationships with other factions successfully. The available diplomatic actions such as trade agreements and non-agression pacts are displayed correctly and are available if the preset conditions are met.

**Result:**

Normal Behaviour



### Exploration 6 — Resource Management

**Action:**

Inspect the faction's available resources, treasury, income, and other relevant economic information. Perform an action that changes the player's available resources and observe the resulting values.

**Observation:**

The faction's available resources are displayed correctly before and after using them, accurately changing to the updated value available to the faction.

**Result:**

Normal Behaviour


### Exploration 7 — Technology & Research

**Action:**

Open the technology or research interface and inspect available technologies. Select an available research option and observe how the interface and campaign state respond.

**Observation:**

Technology and research interface opens correctly with all available technologies being clearly displayed with no unexpected visual issues. The interface responds correctly to selecting a technology to research and remains in the correct updated state before and after saving, exiting, and reloading into the campaign.

**Result:**

Normal Behaviour



### Exploration 8 — Turn Progression

**Action:**

End a campaign turn and observe the transition into the next turn. Monitor changes to the campaign map, faction activity, resources, and available notifications.

**Observation:**

Ending a campaign turn results in all the other factions completing their chosen actions. Changes to the campaign map such as an other faction taking over a neighbouring faction's settlement are also shown correctly. Resource values were updated accordingly. Notifications are also shown correctly relative to previous decisions or new available actions.

**Result:**

Normal Behaviour



### Exploration 9 — Battle Interaction

**Action:**

Enter a battle and explore basic battle controls, including unit selection, movement, attacking, camera controls, and issuing orders.

**Observation:**

Entering a battle displays the correct information about the available armies. The battle controls work appropriately with the camera controls or the army movement. Attacks between soldiers is shown correctly without any visual issues after exploring this through the close camera view. 

**Result:**

Normal Behaviour 



### Exploration 10 — User Interface & Menus

**Action:**

Navigate between different campaign interfaces and menus, including the campaign map, settlement information, character information, diplomacy, technology, and army management interfaces.

**Observation:**

Navigation between different campaign interfaces works smoothly and corrctly. The campaign map successfully allows the player to view between the campaign's factions and other character information such as other faction leaders. Other management interfaces including technology and diplomacy allows for smooth transitions between them with no visual issues.

**Result:**

Normal Behaviour


## Issues Identified

No unexpected behaviours were identified during this exploratory testing session.
The tested campaign and user interface systems, including campaign navigation, army, settlement, resource and character management, diplomacy, technology and research, turn progression, battle interaction, and menus, behaved consistently with their expected functionality.
No observed behaviour required a bug report for this session.
