# BUG-001 — Floating grass assets above soil outside obeservatory environment
## Game
Mafia II: Definitive Edition

## Platform
PlayStation 5

## Date Encountered
16/08/2026

## Environment
- Platform: PlayStation 5
- Game Version: Unkown
- Mode/Level: Chapter 15

## Severity
Minor

## Priority
Low

## Reproducibility
2/5

## Description
During the final chapter, after approaching the observatory and completeing the cutscene where Vito is asked to hand over his weapons, and the first combat sequence begins, multiple grass assets were seen floating above the soil they were intended to be placed on. Issue appears to affect multiple decorative pots and objects in the area.
## Steps to Reproduce
1. Progress to the final chapter of Mafia II: Definitive Edition
2. Approach the Observatory
3. Complete cutscene where Vito is asked to hand over his weapons and proceeds to shoot the mobsters asking him to
4. Observe the environmental decorations and pots in the area

## Expected Result
Grass assets should have been positioned accordingly on their respective decorative pots and objects.

## Actual Result
Grass assets seen floating above their decorative pots and objects.
