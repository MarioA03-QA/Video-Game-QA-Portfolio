# Defect Lifecycle Example

## Overview

Here, I would like to demonstrate that I understand that QA involves more than identifying and reporting bugs. Issues may need to be investigated, assigned, fixed, retested and potentially reopened if the original problem remains.
The example below demonstrates how I used Jira to track a bug through multiple stages of its lifecycle.

## Example Lifecycle

**Report**

The bug was identified during testing and documented in Jira.

**Investigation**

The bug was reviewed and further information or evidence was added where necessary.

**Fix**

The issue was marked as addressed and prepared for further QA testing.

**Retest**

The original reproduction steps were repeated to verify the proposed fix.

**Solving**

The bug was confirmed as fixed and closed.

**If the issue remains:**

The issue would be reopened and returned for further investigation.
