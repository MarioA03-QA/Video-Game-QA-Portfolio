# Jira Board


I created a Jira board to organise and track QA issues identified during my testing.
The board allows individual bugs to be monitored according to their current status, making it easier to understand which issues require investigation, testing or retesting.
The board was created as part of my personal QA portfolio to simulate the type of issue-tracking environment used within a professional development team.
