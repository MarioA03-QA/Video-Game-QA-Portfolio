# Video-Game-QA-Portfolio

This portfolio shows my approach to Quality Assurance testing within video games, focusing on identifying, documenting, and communicating software defects or successes.
The repository contains personal examples of bug reporting, structured test cases, and exploratory testing across multiple games, genres, and platforms including PlayStation 5 and PC.
My testing covers areas such as gameplay mechanics, UI systems, NPC and Player Character behaviour, animation, visual and audio prsentation, combat, inventory systems, save/load systems, and game state behaviour.
Each report documents the testing performed, observations made, expected and actual behaviours, and evidence. The aim is to demonstrate a methodical approach to manual video game Quality Assurance and clear documentation.
This project is part of my development towards a career in vido game Quality Assurance with the goal and ambition of applying these practical skills in a professional QA environment.
Using this portfolio, I intend to demonstrate my practical approach to video game Quality Assurance and my ability to test gameplay systems, identify potential defects, document findings clearly, and communicate results in a structured way.
I am currently developing my QA skills and I am aiming to pursue opportunities in the video game industry, particularly in a Junior QA tester position.
