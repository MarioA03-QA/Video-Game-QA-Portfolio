Major

Medium

Unknown

## Description

After attacking an NPC with a sword, the NPC became stuck in an incorrect animation state.

Following the attack, the NPC's head was tilted backwards and remained fixed in that position. Blood was also continuously visible/suspended from the NPC's chest area, while the NPC remained standing and did not transition into the expected death or fleeing state.

The NPC remained in this state after the attack rather than completing the expected reaction to being struck by the sword.

## Steps to Reproduce

1. Approach an NPC.
2. Attack the NPC with a sword.
3. Observe the NPC's reaction following the attack.

## Expected Result

The NPC should correctly transition into the appropriate reaction to the sword attack, such as dying or fleeing, depending on the NPC's health and the circumstances of the encounter. Character animations and blood effects should also transition correctly.

## Actual Result

The NPC remained standing and became stuck in an incorrect animation state. The NPC's head was tilted backwards and remained fixed in that position, while blood remained visibly suspended from the NPC's chest.
