# BUG-003 — NPC floating above Kuttenberg tournament arena

## Game

Kingdom Come: Deliverance II

## Platform

PlayStation 5

## Date Encountered

Unknown

## Environment

- Platform: PlayStation 5
- Game Version: Unknown
- Mode/Level: Open World
- Location: Kuttenberg Tournament Arena

## Severity

Minor

## Priority

Low

## Reproducibility

Unknown

## Description

Immediately after completing the Kuttenberg Tournament, an NPC was observed floating above the tournament arena.
The NPC was standing in mid-air above the arena and remained completely stationary, with no visible movement or animation.

## Steps to Reproduce

1. Complete the Kuttenberg Tournament.
2. Observe the tournament arena immediately after completing the tournament.
3. Observe the NPCs in and around the arena.

## Expected Result

NPCs should be correctly positioned on the ground or appropriate surfaces within the tournament arena and should display their intended animations and movements.

## Actual Result

An NPC was observed floating above the tournament arena, standing in mid-air and remaining completely stationary.
