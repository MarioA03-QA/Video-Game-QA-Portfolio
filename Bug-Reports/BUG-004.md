# BUG-004 — Markvart von Aulitz's body remains in Kuttenberg after death

## Game

Kingdom Come: Deliverance II

## Platform

PlayStation 5

## Date Encountered

Unknown

## Environment

- Platform: PlayStation 5
- Game Version: Unknown
- Mode/Level: Open World / Post-Mission
- Mission: "Reckoning"
- Location: Kuttenberg Market

## Severity

Minor

## Priority

Medium

## Reproducibility

5/5

## Description

After completing the "Reckoning" mission and completing the optional objective to kill Markvart von Aulitz, Markvart's body was later observed lying on the ground in the market area of Kuttenberg.
The body remained present when returning to Kuttenberg after completing the mission and continued to be present after completing the game.
The body also remained present after closing and reopening the game and after reloading the world.

## Steps to Reproduce

1. Complete the "Reckoning" mission.
2. Complete the optional objective to kill Markvart von Aulitz.
3. Return to the open world.
4. Travel to Kuttenberg.
5. Visit the market area and observe the ground.
6. Observe Markvart von Aulitz's body in the area.

## Expected Result

After Markvart von Aulitz is killed at the designated mission location, his body should no longer appear in an unrelated location in Kuttenberg after the mission has been completed.

The game world should correctly reflect the completed mission and the NPC's final state.

## Actual Result

Markvart von Aulitz's body remained lying on the ground in the Kuttenberg market area after completing the mission.

## Additional Notes

Markvart von Aulitz was killed at a camp located outside Kuttenberg as part of the optional objective during the "Reckoning" mission.

The body was repeatedly observed in the Kuttenberg market area during subsequent visits after the mission and after completing the game.

Markvart's body was seen laying on the ground in the Kuttenberg market area on 5 different deliberate occasions, both after deliberately reloading the map, and restarting the game.
