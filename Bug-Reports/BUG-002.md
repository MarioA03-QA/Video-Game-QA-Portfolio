# BUG-002 — NPC becomes stuck in incorrect animation state after sword attack

## Game

Kingdom Come: Deliverance

## Platform

PlayStation 5

## Date Encountered

09/06/2026

## Environment

- Platform: PlayStation 5
- Game Version: 1.9.7
- Mode/Level: Open World
- Location: Outisde Talmberg

## Severity

Major

## Priority

Low

## Reproducibility

1/1

## Description

After attacking an NPC with a sword, the NPC became stuck in an incorrect animation state.
After attacking, the NPC's head was tilted backwards and remained fixed in that position. Blood was also visible/suspended from the NPC's chest area, while the NPC remained standing and did not transition into the death or fleeing state.
The NPC remained in this state after the attack rather than completing the expected reaction to being struck by the sword until world was reloaded.

## Steps to Reproduce

1. Approach an NPC
2. Attack the NPC with a sword.
3. Observe the NPC's reacon following the attack.

## Expected Result

The NPC should transition into the appropriate reaction to the sword attack such as dying or running away, depending on the NPC's health and the circumstances of the encounter. Character animations and blood effects should also transition correctly.

## Actual Result

The NPC remained standing and became stuck in an incorrect animation state. The NPC's head was tilted backwards and remained fixed in that position, while blood remained visibly suspended from the NPC's chest.
