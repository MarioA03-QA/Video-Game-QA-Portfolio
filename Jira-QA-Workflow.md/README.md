# Jira — QA & Defect Tracking

As part of my Game QA portfolio, I used Jira to simulate practical QA bug-tracking.
I used Jira to create, organise and track bugs from initial discovery and reporting through investigation, retesting and conclusions.
This section demonstrates my understanding of Jira as a QA tool, including bug management and reporting, prioritisation of bugs, workflow management and communicating issues clearly.
